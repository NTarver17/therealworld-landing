Last Updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Real World Portal ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data that you may share with us while visiting our website. This Privacy Policy explains our online information practices, how your information is collected and used, and your privacy rights under applicable data protection laws.

By using or accessing our website, you signify your agreement to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms herein, please do not access or use this site.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways:

  • Voluntary Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, and inquiry details that you voluntarily submit when filling out our contact form or subscribing to updates.
  • Derivative and Technical Data: Information our servers automatically collect when you access the site, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, access times, referring website addresses, and pages viewed directly before and after accessing the site.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We may use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, and other tracking technologies to help customize the site and improve your browsing experience.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookie Disclosure

Third-Party Vendor Notice: Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, commonly known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to this site and other websites on the internet.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on our site, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Visitors may opt out of the use of the DART cookie or personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy or through AboutAds.info.

This website participates in affiliate marketing programs. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you click through and make a purchase, at no additional cost to you.

When you click an affiliate link pointing to external services (such as The Real World checkout portals), third-party cookies or query parameters may be used to track referrals and attribute conversions. We have no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party platforms.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected via our website to:

  • Respond to your customer support inquiries, feedback, or questions.
  • Compile anonymous statistical data and analytics for use internally to improve site performance and user experience.
  • Deliver targeted advertising, coupons, newsletters, and promotions regarding our portal and educational content.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions, monitor against security vulnerabilities, and ensure compliance with our Terms.

6. GDPR & European Privacy Rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, you are entitled to certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • Right of Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.

7. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request that we disclose categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of collected information, and request that we do not sell or share personal information. We do not sell user personal data to third parties.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important. Our website is directed to general audiences aged 18 and older, or minors operating under parental guidance. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete such information.
